Our services

Everything we clean, press and repair at 34 Cold Bath Road. Most items are ready in 2 days, and a 24-hour express service is available for many garments.

Dry cleaning

Suits, coats, dresses, knitwear, silk and delicates. Every item is checked for stains and loose buttons, cleaned with the right process for its fabric, then pressed and finished by hand.

  • Suits, jackets and trousers
  • Winter coats and macs
  • Dresses, skirts and blouses
  • Wool, cashmere and silk
  • Ties and scarves
Ask for a price on WhatsApp

Laundry & service wash

Bring a bag of everyday washing and we will wash, dry and fold it for you. Ideal if you are busy, have no space to dry clothes, or are staying in Harrogate on holiday.

  • Everyday clothes
  • Towels and bed linen
  • Ironing only, if you prefer to wash at home
Ask for a price on WhatsApp

Shirts

Shirts laundered and pressed, returned on a hanger or folded and boxed. A good choice for office shirts week after week.

  • Cotton and cotton-blend shirts
  • Blouses
  • Starch to your liking
Ask for a price on WhatsApp

Alterations & repairs

Hems taken up or let down, zips replaced, garments taken in or let out, and small repairs. Tell us how you want it to fit and we will pin it with you.

  • Trouser and skirt hems
  • Zip replacement
  • Taking in and letting out
  • Buttons and small repairs
Ask for a price on WhatsApp

Wedding & occasion wear

Wedding dresses pressed before the day, then cleaned and boxed afterwards so they keep. We also look after bridesmaid dresses, prom dresses and christening gowns.

  • Wedding dresses
  • Bridesmaid and prom dresses
  • Christening gowns
  • Morning suits
Ask for a price on WhatsApp

Duvets, curtains & bedding

Large items that will not fit, or should not go, in a home washing machine. Feather and down duvets are cleaned and dried thoroughly so the filling stays even.

  • Feather, down and synthetic duvets
  • Pillows
  • Lined and unlined curtains
  • Throws, blankets and table linen
