Named after the Cold Bath spring

Harrogate was built on water. We still treat it with respect.

Our road takes its name from the Cold Bath, a spring of unusually cold, clear water. In the 18th century people bathed in it, and some came to soothe sore eyes. The Great Award of 1778 made it a common watering place, and a bathing house stood on the spot from 1837 until 1974.

The bath house has gone, but the street still washes things properly.

Stain first aid

Spilled something?

Pick what it is to see what to do right now. Then bring it to us, the sooner the better.

WhatsApp us a photoCall for advice

Red wine: first aid

  1. Blot straight away with a clean white cloth. Do not rub.
  2. Rinse from the back of the fabric with cold water.
  3. Avoid salt and hot water, which can set the stain.
  4. Bring it in within a day or two and tell us it was wine.

Tea or coffee: first aid

  1. Blot off as much as you can.
  2. Rinse with cold water from the back of the fabric.
  3. If it had milk in it, keep heat away from it.
  4. Bring it in soon. Old tea and coffee marks turn brown.

Grease or oil: first aid

  1. Do not use water. It spreads oil.
  2. Blot gently with kitchen paper.
  3. Dust with a little cornflour or talc to soak it up, then brush off.
  4. Bring it in. Grease needs a solvent to lift properly.

Make-up or fake tan: first aid

  1. Lift off any excess with the edge of a card.
  2. Do not rub, especially on collars.
  3. Keep it away from heat, including hairdryers and irons.
  4. Bring it in and tell us the product if you know it.

Mud or grass: first aid

  1. Let mud dry completely, then brush off what you can.
  2. For grass, do not wash it at a high temperature.
  3. Bring it in. Grass marks are a dye and need specialist treatment.

Blood: first aid

  1. Use cold water only. Hot water sets blood for good.
  2. Blot from the back of the fabric.
  3. Bring it in as soon as possible.

Questions people ask us

Where is Harrogate Cleaners?

We are at 34 Cold Bath Road, Harrogate HG2 0NA, in the blue shopfront with the Harrogate Cleaners sign. You can call us on 01423 508568 or message us on WhatsApp.

How much does dry cleaning cost?

Every item is priced individually, because the fabric, size and finish all make a difference. Send us a photo on WhatsApp, call us or pop into the shop and we will give you a price straight away.

How long has Harrogate Cleaners been open?

We were established in 1986 and have been cleaning for Harrogate ever since.

How long does dry cleaning take?

Most items are ready in 2 days. We also offer a 24-hour express service. How quickly we can turn an item around depends on the garment and its fabric, so just ask and we will do our best.

Do you offer same-day or express dry cleaning?

We offer a 24-hour express service for many items. Some garments and fabrics need longer, so ask in the shop or call us and we will turn it around as quickly as we can.

Can you clean my wedding dress?

Yes. We clean wedding dresses, including silk, lace and beaded gowns, and can box them in acid-free tissue for long-term storage.

Do you clean duvets and curtains?

Yes. We clean feather, down and synthetic duvets of every size, as well as pillows, throws and curtains.

Do you do alterations and repairs?

Yes. We do hems, zips, taking in and letting out, and small repairs.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Just bring your items to the shop during opening hours. For a wedding dress fitting or large curtain order, a quick call first is helpful.
